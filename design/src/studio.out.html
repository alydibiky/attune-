Studio: how progress is shown while a picture is drawn (item 9)

Each option is shown in 4 states: queued → draft → refining → done. The sample prompt is "red crane lifting a steel beam, sunset". The frames are 320 px. The draft is drawn at 384 px in about 10 s, and the clear 768 px version swaps in later with a 300 ms cross-fade. The chat model stays loaded the whole time.

A · Draft then clear recommended

You see a real picture in about 10 s and can keep it straight away. The clear version swaps in on the same spot, so nothing jumps. This works best with a distilled model that needs few steps. It costs about 10 s more than a single pass.

B · Best of 2

It makes two drafts at once and you tap the better one. Only that one is sharpened. It gives the best results for prompts like the crane one, which go wrong half the time. It needs about 1.3× the memory and time of A. It could be a switch in A ("Best of 2").
