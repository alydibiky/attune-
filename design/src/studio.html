Studio: how progress is shown while a picture is drawn (item 9)

Each option is shown in 4 states: queued → draft → refining → done. The sample prompt is "red crane lifting a steel beam, sunset". The frames are 320 px. The draft is drawn at 384 px in about 10 s, and the clear 768 px version swaps in later with a 300 ms cross-fade. The chat model stays loaded the whole time.
