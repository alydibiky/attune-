Compared with Yazio
From its public store listing and reviews. None of its art is copied here.

What Yazio does

• Its "Diary" home has a calorie ring (eaten / left / burned), 3 macro bars and a water row.
• Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks are cards, each with a ＋.
• To log: ＋ on a meal → search screen → pick the food → portion → add, so 4 taps or more.
• Barcode scan is free; photo logging and many features are behind Pro.
• Fasting timer, recipes and a weekly report sit in other tabs.
• It needs a sign-in and the internet, and its food database comes from the cloud.

Where Attune can win

• 1 tap: recent and frequent meals add straight away (A and C).
• 2 taps: photo → confirm, recognised on the phone and free.
• You can say a meal in Arabic or English ("2 eggs, a loaf of baladi bread and tea").
• Suggestions come from your country's own dishes (Egyptian first).
• It works offline with no account.
• Every add can be undone with a snackbar instead of a confirm dialog.

The rules behind A (they apply to every option)

• The first time you open Fit, it asks 3 questions (sex, age, weight and goal) and then shows Today. The rest of the plan form moves to Profile.
• Tapping a chip adds the food straight away and shows "Added · Undo" for 5 s, with a light haptic tick.
• A photo opens the camera inside the app and shows a skeleton card while the food is recognised. After 15 s you get a fallback: "Type it instead".
• The ring and the bars animate up from their old value (250 ms). The page never reloads.
• Every target is at least 48 px. The bar follows the language direction in RTL, and the numbers use Arabic digits when Arabic is chosen.
• The tabs at the bottom (Today, Recipes, Workouts, Progress) replace today's single long page.
