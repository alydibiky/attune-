Each plan or person is one tile with a ring: how far along it is (Sheikh 2 of 8 paid) or how many days are left. Tiles that need you today have a red border. Tap a tile to answer it or see its history.

Very visual, like phone widgets. You see the state of every plan without reading.

A small reminder (“hear the voice notes”) doesn't fit well as a tile, so reminders would stay in Reminders.
