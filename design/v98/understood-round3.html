M1

Router: the right helper for each question

  • Code first: “maid now 700”, “lunch 150”, “remind me…” are done instantly with no AI.
  • When a sentence is unclear, a tiny model (a few hundred MB, kept loaded) decides what it is, in under a second.
  • Only real questions wake the chat model. A photo goes to the photo reader, a document to the document search.

Limit: the tiny model is only for choosing, never for answering.

M2

Small models helping each other (no big models on phones)

  • Only small models on phones, as you asked. Each one does one job:
    • a tiny router (M1) that stays loaded;
    • a small chat model that answers;
    • a small photo reader, loaded only when there's a photo;
    • a small meaning model that finds the right notes and pages.
  • They work as a relay, one after another, each passing short notes to the next, and code checks numbers, dates and pages at the end.
  • Safety rule: never two models over ~1.5 B loaded together. Attune checks the phone's free memory before loading, and unloads the photo reader right after use, so the app doesn't crash.
  • Each phone level gets its own set (a 6 GB phone gets smaller ones than a 12 GB phone).

Limit: small models are weaker alone. The strength comes from splitting the job and code checking it. I'll measure on your phone before turning it on, and only use models whose licence allows selling the app.

M3

Checker: a second read before you see it

  • For hard questions (contracts, tax, numbers, a long PDF) or when you say “check it”, the answer is read again against its sources.
  • Code checks every number, date and page first; then the small model re-reads only the doubtful sentences, so it stays fast.
  • What changed is shown: “Checked page 12: 2% per day, not 5%. Fixed.”

Limit: adds a few seconds, so only on hard questions or when asked.

M4

Team at the same time (computer app only)

Computer only
  • On a computer, Deep answer runs two models together: a fast one writes a draft while a bigger one checks and improves it.
  • For a long question, each model takes a part, then the parts are joined and checked by code.
  • Phones never do this; they use M2.

Limit: needs a computer with enough memory (16 GB+). Attune checks and falls back to one model if not.

Teach Attune from your notifications

Your example: Life360 says “Nada · Completed a 13 km drive”. You tell Attune “Nada is my wife”, and from then on it understands every notification about her. These four parts can go together. Pick the ones you want.

L1

Talk to any notification

Recommended
  • Tap a notification in Recall or on Home and type or say what it means: “Nada is my wife”, “this is my car's tracker”, “Yazio is just reminders”.
  • Attune saves it in People (Nada = wife) or as a rule for that app.
  • From then on it uses it everywhere: Home, Chat, voice.
