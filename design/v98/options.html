A new idea as strong as Agents (I)

Each one uses what only Attune has together: the phone's notifications, an offline model, your money, people and documents. A cloud app can't read all of that, and iPhones don't allow it.

I1 Autopilot: notifications do themselves Recommended

Every notification is understood and turned into the thing it means, waiting for one tap.

  • CIB “debited EGP 1,250 at Carrefour” → expense, category Food, ready.
  • Amazon “Arriving Thursday” → a follow-up on Thursday, and it closes itself on “Delivered”.
  • Ahmed: «ابعتلي الورق بكرة» → to-do tomorrow with “send Ahmed the papers”.
  • Missed call from a client twice → “call back” on top of Home.
  • Vodafone “bill due 15th” → a bill in People & plans.

Why it's hard to copy: needs notification access + an offline model + your own money, people and to-dos in one app. Google / Apple assistants don't write into your own ledger.

I2 Paperwork Guardian (Egypt first)

Photograph any official paper: car licence, crane inspection certificate, passport, insurance, ID, lease. Attune reads the expiry, reminds you 30 days before, and lists exactly what to bring to renew it (which office, which papers, fees), then fills the form from your details.

Why it's hard to copy: the renewal steps per country (Egypt's traffic unit, Saudi visa) are a pack Attune owns; it works offline and keeps the papers private.

I3 Promise Radar

Every promise made by you or to you (WhatsApp, calls you note, notifications) becomes one list: “Mahmoud said he'd send the crane invoice on Monday”. When it's late, Attune writes the polite reminder in your Franco or Arabic style and sends it on one tap.

Why it's hard to copy: it joins Chat X-Ray, message alerts and Messages scheduling, which you already have.

I4 Ask my month

One page at the end of every month, written by the model from everything on the phone: what you spent and why it went up, who still owes you, what you promised, what the agents saved you, which papers expire next month. You can ask it anything (“why was October more expensive?”) and it answers from your own data.

Why it's hard to copy: only works when money, people, notifications and agents live in one offline app.

New ideas not in the app yet (I5–I7)

I5 Handshake: spoken deals become written Recommended

Most of your deals are spoken: a crane rented for 3 days at 18,000, a deposit of 5,000, return by Thursday. Say it to Attune (or record the talk, with consent). Offline, it writes the terms: who, what, price, dates, penalties. It sends both sides a clean summary on WhatsApp to confirm with one reply, then tracks it: payments become boxes, the return date becomes a follow-up, and late days are counted.

Hard to copy: needs offline Arabic and Franco speech, your contacts, Money and Follow-ups together. It is the paper trail Egyptian business usually doesn't have.

I6 Five seconds after every call

When a call ends, a small bubble: “Anything to keep?”. You say «هيبعت الفلوس الخميس» and it becomes a follow-up with that person's name. No recording, only your own words. After a month, Attune knows everything people promised you on the phone.

Hard to copy: Android only, needs the phone's call state plus an offline assistant. iPhone apps can't do it.

I7 One ledger, two phones

Sheikh's 19,500 in instalments: you show a QR code, he scans it with his Attune, and you both see the same plan. When he pays he taps “paid”, you get “confirm 2,500 received?”, and it's ticked on both phones. No arguments about what was paid. Works over Wi-Fi or a link, with no server keeping your money data.

Hard to copy: each person you share with brings the app to someone new (it spreads by itself), and it's private.
