# Attune — the whole app, tested 7 ways (27 Oct 2026, v8.6)

Ali asked for seven reviews: a sceptical tester who uses it for two months, a normal user, a marketer, an innovator, a UI/UX
designer, a logic tester, and a Play Store launch expert. Rule kept: **objective bugs were fixed now**; anything that is a
matter of taste (layout, names, what to remove) is listed as **options for Ali to pick** — nothing subjective was changed.

**How it was tested (not guessed):**
- The real app (v8.6) at phone size (390×844), as a customer sees it (release mode, no developer switches), in **English and
  Arabic**: 17 screens + the sidebar + Plans (screenshots in the session).
- **~80 real-life sentences** typed into Home, the food reader and the agents (money, debts, reminders, appointments, blood sugar,
  30 Egyptian / global foods, buying and watching) — each output read and judged.
- The whole automated suite (190+ unit files, 150+ browser tests) — every failure investigated.
- Storage growth over months, simulated from the real saved shapes.
- What could **not** be tested here: answer quality with a real AI model (this box has only a tiny test model — run
  `tests/trials/max.mjs` on your chosen model), the watch, the mic and real shop pages. Those need your phone.

## 2 · A normal user (first week)
1. **"No model" sits on every page** — a new person doesn't know what a model is or that the AI needs a one-time download.
2. **I couldn't find Homework, Travel, Learn or Maps** — the sidebar has Money · Follow-ups · Business · Health · Saved · Agents ·
   Tools · Assistants · Projects; Students, Daily, Go and Create aren't there (only through search).
3. **Five different input boxes** (Home, Money, Health, Follow-ups, Agents) — each understands different things.
4. Some pages have **no title in the top bar** (Health, Students, Go); Agents' bar says "Attune".
5. **Business looks like another app** (its own back button and bottom bar).
6. Follow-ups opens with **"All 0 · Money 0 · Reminders 0 · Dates 0 · Guardian 0"** and a row of underlined links that wraps.
7. Travel shows **"5 KB"** next to each country and **"Exchange rates: never"** (sounds broken).
8. Home says **"See all" twice**.
9. Good: the Home box really does things (expense, debt, reminder, sugar reading — each with Undo); Arabic reads naturally.

## 3 · The marketer (making people pay, and pay longer)
- **Lead with outcomes, not feature names.** The paywall says "Deal Check & Chat X-Ray" — words a new user doesn't know yet.
  Better: "Know who owes you · Never forget a payment · Lose weight by photo · An assistant that finds and buys for you".
- **Gate capabilities, not answers.** Free = 15 answers a day, but answers run on the phone and cost you nothing — the limit
  feels arbitrary. Keep answers free; make Pro = agents that act, unlimited photo meals, Business exports, the weekly reports.
- **Start the 7-day trial at the first real use** (first AI answer or first logged item), not at install — today, trial days
  burn while people download the model.
- **Lifetime always on sale undercuts the yearly plan.** MONETIZATION.md says "launch offer only" — show Lifetime only for the
  first 200 buyers / 30 days.
- **Three hero jobs for the store page and the first screen:** Money + Follow-ups · Fit & Food · Agents. Everything else lives
  under Tools ("more tools") so the app doesn't feel cluttered.
- Prices: EGP 299 / 2,499 / 5,999 are reasonable for what's inside; once you have reviews and demo videos, consider the yearly
  plan at EGP 1,999 for the first month after launch (growth beats margin at the start).
- Store listing in Arabic first, 6 screenshots = the 3 hero flows, one 30-second video ("I typed 'Ahmed owes me 500'…").

## 4 · The innovator (what would make it 10× more valuable)
1. **Attune Saves** (parked by you) — a monthly receipt: "Attune saved you EGP 3,420" (agents' price drops, double charges,
   cancelled subscriptions). Best retention and marketing hook in the app.
2. **Camera-first**: point the camera at anything — a receipt → Money, a plate → Fit, a bill → Follow-ups, a document → Saved,
   a menu → Translate. One button, the app decides (it already has every reader).
3. **Weekly life card** every Friday: money, food, follow-ups, what agents found — one push notification people open.
4. **Price history from the agents** — every check is kept; show the graph and "best time to buy".
5. **Family space** (Sync is built): shared expenses, family medicines, one grocery to-do.
6. **Optional cloud boost** for heavy jobs (long research, big documents) as a paid add-on — the phone model stays private and
   free, the hard 5 % gets fast and smart.
7. **Home-screen widget with the box** — type "lunch 150" without opening the app.

## 5 · The UI/UX designer
- **One box component everywhere** (the Home box), so every page understands the same sentences and learns the same way.
- **One page shell**: title always in the bar, the same menu/back, Business inside it.
- **Replace the "No model" pill** with a single first-run card ("Get Attune's brain — 1.2 GB, once, on Wi-Fi") and show the
  model state only where AI is about to be used.
- **Empty states teach with one tap** ("Try: spent 250 on lunch" as a button that fills the box).
- **Density**: cards have large radius and padding — about three cards per screen; trimming ~20 % shows twice as much.
- Fixed now: the Money "+" covered the Money Report link; one-tap choices; Arabic strings for the new parts.
- Small: in Arabic, "وفّر 30%" shows as "%30".

## 6 · The logic tester (code, data, AI)
- **Code-first readers are the app's strength**: of 36 Home sentences, 27 did the right thing before the fixes and 31 after
  (still missed: a medicine not set up, a transfer, sleep, a passport renewal, "coffee 85"); of 30 foods, 24 → 29 correct;
  agents 10 → 11 of 12 (flights can't be watched; vague ones like "buy me something" are asked about, never guessed).
- **The food table is the weak spot** for drinks and modern dishes (frappuccino, oat milk, cheesecake portions were wrong) —
  suggest a monthly "top 100 foods people typed that weren't found" list (kept on the phone, shown to you in developer mode).
- **AI answers**: not measurable here; the trial scripts exist — run them on the model you ship.
- **Storage**: see 1 — move Fit and Money to IndexedDB.
- **Tests**: the suite is large and caught 4 of my own regressions this session — keep running all of it before every push.

## 7 · The Play Store launch expert
**Blockers (fix before submitting):**
1. ✅ **AI-generated content policy**: Play requires a way to report offensive AI output inside the app — there was none.
   **Added now**: every answer has "Report this answer" (offensive / harmful / sexual / false / other). **You must put a
   support e-mail in `web-src/billing.js` → `SUPPORT_EMAIL`** so reports reach you.
2. **Draw over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)** for the quiet orb — Play reviews this permission strictly and often rejects it.
   Recommend: remove it from the Play build (the tap-to-talk notification already works without it).
3. **Foreground services**: microphone (wake word), dataSync (books), specialUse (AI work) — each needs a declaration with a
   short video in the Play Console.
4. **Health Connect**: a declaration form per data type (steps, calories, distance, heart rate, exercise, skin and basal
   temperature) + the privacy policy listing them.
5. **Notification access + SMS notifications**: Data safety form — "messages are read on the device, not shared"; the in-app
   disclosure screens already exist.
6. **The keyboard (IME)**: Play requires a clear disclosure that it doesn't collect what's typed.
7. **Video Downloader**: YouTube / TikTok / Instagram are blocked in code (good), but "downloader" apps get extra review —
   recommend leaving it out of the first Play release.
8. **Huawei**: register once (HUAWEI_SETUP.md) — not needed for Play approval.

**Ready:** target Android 17 (API 37), signed release flow, Play Billing products, privacy policy (EN + AR), no accessibility
service, purchases never automatic (the buy agent stops at payment).

**Launch plan:** closed test 14 days (≥12 testers, required for new personal accounts) → open test with the 3 hero flows →
production in Egypt first, then the Gulf.
