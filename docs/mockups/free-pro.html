What is free, what is Pro, and how Attune teaches people to use it

You asked for a smart split: Free good enough that people fall in love and tell friends, and Pro clearly the easier, faster, smoother way to organise everything. You also said that even you find it hard to use every feature, so Pro must guide people to set up their life with Attune, with pop-ups in the app and a place in Settings. Pick one option in each part (tick several to join them) and add a note anywhere. Nothing is built before your picks.

The idea behind my recommendation
  • Free = you do it, Attune keeps it. Everything you type or say works without limits: money, reminders, debts, lists, dates and Chat. That builds the daily habit and the good reviews.
  • Pro = Attune does it for you, everywhere. It reads your bank messages, runs your rules, watches prices, plans your morning, syncs to the computer and keeps every year. Pro sells time saved, not unlocked buttons.
  • The guide is part of Pro. People pay for the result (an organised life), not for features. The guide gets each person there in their first week.
  • The current prices stay (Egypt: EGP 99 a month · EGP 799 a year; other countries by region). Hadith search, prayers and the emergency card stay free for everyone, as you decided.
