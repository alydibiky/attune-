  1. A dedicated place: "Passwords" in the side menu (and on the computer's sidebar). It holds every account — the ones you add and the ones in Papers today.
  2. Two views, one switch: Toggles like Notion (each account folded; tap ▸ to open it) and All together (everything open in one list). Attune remembers the one you used last. A search box on top.
