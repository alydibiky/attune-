What I understood
  1. On a computer, Attune can wear the dark glass look: a deep burgundy-to-black background with frosted cards (real blur on a computer).
  2. Home's centre is this week, Monday to Sunday: each day's things as cards — the time (or "due" / "renews" / "arrives"), what it is, the amount. Today is marked. ‹ Today › moves the week.
  3. It is filled only from what Attune already has: reminders, payments and bills due, Follow-ups, people to call, project dates, papers, agents' deliveries, trips (a trip is one bar across its days) — and the phone's own calendar if you allow it there. No new page in the side menu.
  4. A tap on a card opens it on the right (the list + details layout you picked), never a phone pop-up.
  5. Beside the week: safe to spend, agents, latest payments.
  6. Colours tell the kind: pink = money, green = people, amber = home & orders, blue = projects.
