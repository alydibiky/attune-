Projects and My things

Tick what you want, add a note on any option, then press Copy my picks and paste it to me. Nothing is built until you pick. My recommendation is marked on each group.

Is Projects bad on the phone?

Partly. The phone's AI can only read a few pages of a project's files at once, so "ask anything about all my files" is slow and weak there. The computer reads far more and is fast.

The other parts of a project are light and work fine on the phone: its tasks, its dates, its money, its people, and the summary of what was decided. So my advice is W2: keep a light Projects on the phone and put the heavy part (files and asking about them) on the computer only.

P · Connect a project to the rest of the app

Today a project is a closed folder. These link it to what you already use every day. Pick any number. I recommend P1 + P2 + P3.

P1

A project's dates show in Follow-ups

Recommended
  • A date said in the project's chats or files ("the visa appointment is Thursday", "tiles arrive on the 24th") goes into Follow-ups, with the project's name on it.
  • Tap it and the project opens on that line.
  • Works the same on the phone and the computer.

P2

A project has its own money

Recommended
  • Give it a budget once ("Umrah budget 60,000").
  • Payments that name it ("paid 6,000 hotel deposit for the Umrah") are counted for it, from Money. Nothing is typed twice.
  • The project shows spent, left, and the last payments. Money shows the project's name on those lines.

P3

A project knows its people

Recommended
  • People named in the project ("the contractor Hany", "Noudy") are linked to their People cards.
  • Their page shows "In projects: Flat Maadi", and the project shows who is in it, with what they owe or are owed.
  • If a name could be two people, Attune asks which one, as it does now.

Recommended
