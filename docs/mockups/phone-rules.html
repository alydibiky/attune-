When should “when I …” rules fire?

Your rules like “when I connect to my car, open Maps”, “when I start charging at night, plan tomorrow” and “when I get home on Wi-Fi, remind me to call Mama” need the phone to notice the moment. Android lets a closed app wake up when a Bluetooth device connects — but NOT when charging starts or Wi-Fi joins, unless the app keeps a small permanent notification. Pick how you want it.

What doesn't change in any option
  • A rule never fires wrongly: if the phone can't notice something, the rule says so (“works when Attune is open”) instead of pretending.
  • Each trigger gets its 10,000-case test (the right device, the right Wi-Fi, a car that connects twice in a minute counts once, nothing at 3 am unless you said so).
  • Bluetooth needs Android's “Nearby devices” permission once, so Attune can see your car's name.
