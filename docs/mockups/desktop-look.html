L1Calm workspace Recommended

Like Linear, Notion or Things: a soft side menu, lots of white space, thin borders, one accent colour, a search-and-ask bar (Ctrl K) on top. Lists and details side by side.

+ Clean and professional; easy to read for hours; fits the list + details you picked.− Quiet: less "wow" at first glance.

L2Bento dashboard

Like the iPhone widgets or Arc: Home is a grid of coloured tiles of different sizes, a slim dark icon bar on the side. Money becomes tiles with a chart on top and the list underneath.

+ The most eye-catching; everything important visible at once.− Busy with a lot of data; a different feel from the phone.

L3Three panes, like Outlook

An icon rail, then the list, then the details, and a narrow "This week" column on the right that always shows money, days and agents.

+ Most efficient on big screens; nothing is ever a click away.− Tight on small laptops; feels like a work tool.

L4Dark glass

A deep burgundy-to-black background with frosted-glass cards, like the Windows 11 and macOS widget look. Dark by default, with a light version.

+ Modern and premium; very appealing in the evening.− Glass effects cost smoothness on weaker computers; harder to read in daylight.
