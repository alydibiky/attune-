The idea behind all of it الفكرة كلها

Attune already reads a lot on the phone: bank and wallet notifications, messages, calls, Follow-ups, the calendar and your typing. Automation (أتمتة) means one engine turns what it reads into actions you approve once. These three parts are the same for every option below.

When (the trigger)a time, a place you arrive at or leave, the car's Bluetooth, charging at night, a message from a person, a payment, a date in Follow-ups
If (the check)only on weekdays, only above 1,000, only if not done yet, only if nobody replied
Do (the action)remind, add a to-do or list item, log money, move to a pot, open a page or app, draft a WhatsApp, silence the phone
