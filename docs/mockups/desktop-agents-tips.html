Your computer list, 17 Nov

Some of your points were plain bugs; I fixed them and added tests. Three need your eye first: how the computer app should look, how an agent shows the listing it found, and how tips appear. Tick what you want and add a note. The bar at the bottom copies your picks.

Already fixed (no choice needed)

  • "36 tools" opened 3: the Tools page now lists every tool by group under the families, and the Home card counts exactly what the page shows.
  • Claude's sign-in email in Dates: sign-in links, login codes, "verify your email" and password resets are never dates or subscriptions now. The wrong entry already in your Dates is removed. Tested on 10,000 messages in many languages, plus a held-out set: 100%.
  • Glitchy while the model loads: the whole app was redrawn every second just to move the "Loading 66 s" counter. Now only the counter ticks.
  • Slow load on the computer: the app remembers the way that started your model last time and tries it first, so a failed graphics-chip attempt is not repeated. The Engine sheet now names the real step ("Loading the model onto the chip", "Warming up") and the log records the load time.
