CR · When the app has a problem (crash reports)

Today, if Attune closes by itself, nobody knows why. These options keep a short note on the phone. Nothing is ever sent by itself: you see the report first and send it yourself (WhatsApp or e-mail) to the support address. The report has the app version, the phone model and the error lines, and none of your messages, money or people.
