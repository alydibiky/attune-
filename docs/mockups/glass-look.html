The glass look, round 2

You liked L4 (dark glass) but no look yet. Here it is again, now with a light version in your phone's own colours (the burgundy, the soft pink, the warm grey ground), the same look on the phone, and three ways to put a week calendar on the computer. Tick what you like, write what to change.

My honest view before you pick
  • Calendar: worth it on a computer — but only as a week view of what Attune already knows (reminders, Sheikh's instalments, the maid, Follow-ups, papers, trips, project dates, agent dates). Without your work meetings it can look empty: Attune does not read Google / Outlook today. On the phone it can read the phone's own calendar if you allow it.
  • Glass on the phone: the real "frosted" blur costs smoothness and battery on mid-range phones. On the phone I would draw the glass look without live blur (it looks almost the same). On a computer the real blur is fine.
  • Dark by default? Easier at night, harder in daylight. I would follow the phone / computer setting (dark at night, light in the day), with your choice kept.

Where the week calendar lives on the computer

C1The centre of Home Recommended

As drawn in G1 / G2: this week on Home, filled from Attune's own dates (and the phone's calendar if you allow it). A tap on any block opens it in the right pane. Nothing new in the side menu.

+ No new feature to learn; you see the week every time you open the app.− Home gets busier.

C2A Calendar page in the side menu (Month · Week · Day)

Its own page with the classic three views, drag a block to move it (a reminder, a payment date, a project task).

+ A full calendar, familiar from Outlook / Google.− One more page; empty-looking without your work meetings.

C3A thin "This week" column on the right of every page

Always visible beside Money, Projects, Agents… — the next seven days as a list.

+ Never out of sight.− Takes width from every page.
