What I understood

  1. One "Add passwords" screen in Papers with two ways: Paste a whole page (PW1) or choose the .csv exported from a password app (PW2). On the computer the same, plus dropping the file on the window.
  2. The paste is split by its toggles / headings; in each part Attune finds the site, email, user name, password, PIN and notes — English, Arabic and Franco labels («الباسورد», «باس», "pw").
  3. Both ways end on one check list: already-saved accounts are kept once, an unsure one is marked so you fix only that one. Nothing is saved before you tap Save.
  4. Every password is saved as an Account paper, locked by the fingerprint like a CVC; Undo removes the whole batch. Nothing leaves the phone.
  5. After a .csv, Attune offers to delete the file (it is plain text).
  6. Tested on 10,000 hard pages and 10,000 files (every app's column names, quotes, commas and line breaks inside a password, Arabic, blanks).
