Attune widgets

Your home screen, with things you can do right there: tick, add, pay, mark done, snooze, open the exact page. Pick the widgets you want, the way the list widget should work, and the look. Tick an option, add a note, then tap Copy my picks at the bottom.

"I want widgets for most things in the app in a smart way, and they must be interactive, not like this, so I can do things without opening the app."

What every widget does, whatever you pick

  • Taps work on the home screen. Ticks, Done, Paid, Add and Snooze happen right there. The app doesn't open, and Money, your lists and Follow-ups change at once.
  • Typing happens in a small box over the home screen. Android doesn't allow a text field inside a widget, so + Add or the mic opens a small box (keyboard or voice) over your wallpaper. Attune itself doesn't open.
  • Live: each widget updates when something changes in the app, and every 30 minutes.
  • Every widget in all 20 languages, and right-to-left in Arabic.
