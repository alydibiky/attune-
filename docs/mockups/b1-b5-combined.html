B1Tap where an answer came from

تدوس على مصدر الإجابة وتفتح الحاجة نفسها
What I understood
  1. Every answer worked out from your own data (the green "From your own data" card, in Chat and on Home) ends with up to 3 chips, one per thing it used: 💳 a Money entry, 📝 a note, 📅 a date or reminder, 👤 a People & plans item. "+N more ›" opens the full list.
  2. A chip opens that exact thing. A Money entry opens Money with the row lit up and its edit sheet open (edit, change category, delete). A note opens it in Mind. A date opens it in Dates.
  3. A total ("2,480 on Food") gets one chip that opens Money filtered to those entries.
  4. Answers written by the model from your notes keep the "From your Mind: …" line, but each title becomes a tappable chip.
  5. Same on the computer, where the chip opens the page beside the chat. In all 20 languages, light and dark.

B5Test every page, guided

اختبار كل صفحة على موبايلك خطوة بخطوة
What I understood
  1. It lives in Settings › Test every page, and in the side menu's search as "test". It's for you now, before launch. Later it can be hidden for regular users.
  2. It walks through every page on the Tools list (23 today), with one real thing to try per page. The things to try use your own data where you have it (your barber, your bank SMS) and examples otherwise, plus what the page should do.
  3. For each page: ✓ Works, ✗ Wrong (with a note and an optional screenshot) or Skip. Open … jumps to the page and you come back to the same step.
  4. Progress is kept, so you can stop and carry on later. Each run is stamped with the app version.
  5. At the end, Share the list sends the results through your share sheet, as text plus your screenshots, to WhatsApp, e-mail, or here. Nothing is ever sent by itself.
  6. On the computer it's the same steps in a side panel beside the page being tested. In all 20 languages.
