Your picks: CG-3 with your book idea, WK-2 and WK-3. These are the real screens with what I understood. OK them or write a note, then copy. I build after your OK.

What I understood
  • CG-3: a book goal counts from the +1 on its card and from saying it (“finished another book”, «خلصت كتاب») in Chat or the Home box, with Undo. A Sunday line only after 3 weeks with nothing counted.
  • Your book idea: each +1 can become the book itself: title and cover, the plot in your words, your review and stars, what you learned. All optional, it saves with just the title.
  • Its PDF or e-book: attach it and it goes into the library Attune already reads on the phone (Ask a PDF). Later Chat answers from it, with the page: “what does chapter 3 say about habits?”, “what did I learn from Atomic Habits?” (your notes + the book).
  • Every goal you count works the same way (runs, gym visits, courses), the book details only for books.
  • WK-2 + WK-3: the week starts on your country’s day (Saturday in Egypt and the Gulf, Sunday in the US, Monday in Europe), and Settings → Language & region lets you change it. Money, Your week and every report follow it.
  • Tested with 10,000 sentences (“finished a book”, «خلصت كتاب», Franco, the 20 languages) and 10,000 weeks per country, checked by code.
