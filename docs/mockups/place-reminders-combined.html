The combined screen — what I understood

You picked P-2: place reminders work first in the APK you install yourself; the Play version says “Coming soon” until you decide to add them there. Tick OK (or write what to change) and send it — then I build.

What I understood
  • The first time you make a place reminder, Attune shows its own short explanation, then Android asks — you pick “Allow all the time”. If you pick “Only while using the app”, Attune says the reminder can't ring with the app closed and shows how to change it.
  • You choose the place from your saved places or “Where I am now” + a name, and “When I arrive” / “When I leave” — the screen you already OK'd (T4).
  • It rings once per arrival: within about 150 m, two readings in a row (a GPS jump or driving past doesn't count), and on leaving after 200 m.
  • Your location stays on the phone — nothing is sent anywhere.
  • The Play build doesn't even ask for the permission (so nothing for Google to review); there the place option says “Coming soon”.
