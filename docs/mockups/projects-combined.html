Projects, My things and where everything is

Two parts. Part 1 is what I understood from your picks, drawn as one design: tick OK or write what to change. Part 2 is the map of where every feature lives today, plus three ways to make them easier to find. Then press Copy my picks and paste it to me.

Part 1 · What I understood (P1 P2 P3 P4 W2 + T1 in Follow-ups)

  • My things moves into Follow-ups as its own chip, next to Money, Reminders and Dates. Each thing's price is linked to its payment in Money: tap the price and the payment opens. If the payment isn't in Money yet, saving the receipt adds it there (with Undo). The warranty end stays on the Follow-ups list with a warning before it. (Your T1 note.)
  • P1: dates from a project's chats and files appear in Follow-ups with the project's name on them; a tap opens the project.
  • P2: a project can have a budget; payments that name it are counted from Money. The project shows spent and left.
  • P3: the people in a project are linked to their People cards, both ways.
  • P4: share a message, file or photo to Attune and pick a project; or say "add this to Flat Maadi" in the Home box.
  • W2: on the phone a project shows tasks, dates, money, people and "Decided / Still open". Files and asking about them are on the computer only. Everything syncs.
